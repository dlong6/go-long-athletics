Meet the Coach

About Coach Long

Coach Long on the sideline, 2025

Hey, I'm Coach Devin Long, and I started Go Long Athletics in 2026. Young athletes, especially pitchers, are undergoing arm surgeries at an alarming rate, and I want to help reverse that trend by teaching healthy, repeatable motions. I prioritize flexibility and efficiency over max effort, and we never throw through pain.

Arm pain is a sympton of either mechanical inefficiency, from overuse, or both. We can also help athletes with existing arm pain correct their motions, so they can continue throwing unhindered.

I still throw every day, whether it's rec sports, coaching sessions, or fetch with my dog. Despite the high mileage on my arm, I've never had a throwing-related injury, and I throw harder now than I did at 21, all thanks to mechanics.
