What We Work On

Four Pillars of Elite QB Play

Hip-Shoulder Separation

Generate torque from the ground up. The best QBs — from Mahomes to Montana — create enormous rotational energy between their hips and shoulders. That's where real velocity comes from, not arm strength alone. We build the movement patterns that make this automatic.

Footwork & Platform

Your feet set up every throw. We build repeatable, explosive footwork patterns that put you in a powerful position before the ball ever leaves your hand — whether you're working a 3-step drop, buying time in the pocket, or stepping up on a scramble drill.

Release Point Consistency

A clean, consistent release point is the difference between elite accuracy and a tipped ball. We identify mechanical flaws on film and use targeted drills to ingrain a repeatable release until it's instinct — not thought.

Film Analysis

Every session is recorded. You see exactly what's happening in your mechanics — no guesswork. We review the film together and turn observations into actionable adjustments. What gets measured gets fixed.

How It Works

A Typical QB Session

01

Film Review

We open with recent footage — game tape or clips from your last session — and identify 1-2 specific mechanical issues to target that day. No distractions, no generalities.

02

Warm-Up & Flexibility Routine

A structured arm-care and hip mobility sequence designed to protect the shoulder and elbow while maximizing range of motion. This isn't optional — it's part of your mechanics.

03

Technical Drills

Targeted reps for today's focus area — hip rotation, footwork patterns, release mechanics, or accuracy routes. Max 4 athletes per session means you get real coaching, not a number.

04

Radar & Session Wrap

We close with radar measurements and a filmed set. You leave knowing your velocity, what you improved, and exactly what to work on before the next session.
